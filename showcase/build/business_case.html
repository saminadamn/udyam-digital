03Value for the owner

From a visit and 17.6 days to one session on a phone, in their own language.

Discover

Six questions

An advisor in 8 Indian languages, by voice or tap. The GSTIN is checked live at question 3.

Buy

One monthly price

A reason for every item, add-ons that toggle, and a line comparing it with what they pay today.

KYC

Checked on upload

Consent first. Registry lookups pre-fill details; 13 checks catch a stale bill now, not days later.

Go live

Dates, not chasing

Install books itself. A tracker and WhatsApp updates; a named person calls if anything runs late.

8 daystarget, lead to live

The owner never waits for a callback or a PDF quote. Eligibility, quantities and prices come from a rules engine, so no one is sold a bundle they can't use. The AI only words the explanation.

How it's calculated

  • Activations = leads × conversion (7.4% → 11%)
  • Spend = activations × cost per activation (Rs 15,053 → Rs 8,000)

07Rollout

Pilot narrow, prove the five metrics, then scale to the field force and micro shops.

0–3 months · Pilot
  • Professional offices in 2 tier-1 cities
  • Real GSTN / Udyam lookups through a GSP or KYC vendor
  • DigiLocker pull for documents; Aadhaar e-sign for the order form
  • Name a grievance officer; publish the privacy notice
3–6 months · Scale
  • WhatsApp Business API in production; real SMS OTP
  • Postgres with keys in a cloud KMS; key rotation
  • Partner and field-force portal: scored leads, assisted mode, shared credit
  • Micro-retail wave with a lean bundle
6+ months · Later
  • Plug into a DPDP consent manager
  • Voice-first flow on WhatsApp
  • Retrain propensity on live outcomes; monitor bias by segment and language
  • Single bill and one support number across products

Go / no-go after the pilot: conversion ≥ 11%, cost per activation ≤ Rs 8,000, KYC completion 68.3% → 85%, first response to escalations within 4 business hours.

The field force is part of the plan: the app hands them scored leads, not fewer deals8 / 10

09The ask

A plan in minutes. A target of 8 days to live, not 17.6.

Proven in the build

  • One-session journey on a phone, language to live, with real uploads
  • Zero ineligible bundles: the rules engine has the final say
  • Bad documents caught at upload with a specific fix (12/12 KYC verdicts)
  • Late steps escalate on their own to the right agent
  • 192 automated tests and 15 judge scenarios pass

To validate in the pilot

  • How many drops self-serve recovers (32% direct to 69% including partly)
  • A 50/50 self-serve / assisted mix, with assisted deals 40% cheaper
  • KYC completion 68.3% → 85%
  • Whether regional-language voice closes the quote gap

Decision requested: approve a 3-month pilot with professional offices in two tier-1 cities, measured on the five metrics, with real registry lookups through a KYC vendor.
