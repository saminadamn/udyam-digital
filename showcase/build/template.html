01 Data · where the leads leak

The quote is the bottleneck, and speed loses more deals than price.

Funnel, 5,000 leads → 368 live

Bar length = leads remaining · label = % of the previous stage that got through

Why 4,632 leads dropped

  1. The quote is the bottleneck. Only 43.6% of assessed leads accept it, 32% for micro businesses. An instant, plain price is the most valuable screen.
  2. Speed beats price. 41.2% of drops are about reach and speed; 23.9% about price or understanding; only 5.9% about paperwork.
  3. The advisor must enforce the rules. 56 of 368 customers were sold bundles the catalogue forbids.

03 Build · architecture

One API, six services, one database, and a person in the loop.

Tap the diagram to open it full size.

Personal data protected. The API masks IDs in responses and logs; KYC encrypts documents and ID numbers; SQLite encrypts PII columns; documents auto-delete.
A person steps in. KYC review, late installs, a stuck advisor or "talk to a person" land in the agent queue with full context.
Swappable providers. LLM, registries, Twilio and Sarvam/Bhashini sit behind one interface each; mocks run the demo.
Stack. Next.js 15 · FastAPI · SQLAlchemy + SQLite · Tesseract OCR · scikit-learn · Docker on Render, Vercel.

06 Impact

What a 6-month pilot could change, per quarter of leads.

Try the pilot targets

Five pilot metrics: baseline → target

Proven in the build
  • Zero ineligible bundles: the rules engine has the final say.
  • One-session journey on a phone, language to live, with real uploads.
  • Bad documents caught at upload with a specific fix.
  • Late steps escalate on their own to the right agent.
To validate in the pilot
  • How many drops self-serve recovers: 32% (direct) to 69% (incl. partly).
  • A 50/50 self-serve / assisted mix, assisted deals 40% cheaper.
  • KYC completion 68.3% → 85%; first response to escalations within 4 business hours.
  • Whether regional-language voice closes the quote gap (40% vs 45%).
