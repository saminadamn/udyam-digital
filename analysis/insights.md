# Udyam Digital: top 10 findings

Source: smb_leads.csv (5,000 leads, Apr-Jun 2026), product catalogue and mock registries. Tables behind every number are in 02_signals.md.

1. The quote step loses the most leads: only 44% of assessed leads accept a quote, and just 32% of micro businesses do.
2. Reach and speed cause 41.2% of all drops (unreachable, no time for a visit, visit not scheduled, slow quote, late install), more than price does.
3. A self-serve digital journey fully fixes 32% of today's drops and partly fixes another 37%, so roughly 69% of lost leads are in reach.
4. Leads with a GSTIN activate at 9.6% against 2.0% without one, a 4.7x gap that makes the GSTIN lookup the most valuable first question.
5. Referrals convert at 14.8% for Rs 4,099 per activation, while field sales converts at 7.3% for Rs 24,335, about 6x the cost (blended Rs 15,053).
6. WhatsApp-campaign and website-form leads are the cheapest to generate but 44% and 34% of their drops are unreachable or wrong numbers, because they wait for a callback that never lands.
7. 56 of 368 activated customers (15%) were sold a product the catalogue rules forbid them, including GST-only products sold on cancelled or missing GSTINs.
8. Customers who chased status once churned at 6%, those who chased five or more times at 28%, with NPS falling from 7.5 to 6.0.
9. Small manufacturers convert best at 12.8% and Micro retail & services worst at 4.1%, and 2-product bundles churn at 24% against 14% for 3-6 products.
10. Regional-language leads accept the quote 40% of the time against 45% for Hindi and English, with Tamil (4.3%), Punjabi (4.2%) and Kannada (5.1%) converting at well under the 7.4% average.
